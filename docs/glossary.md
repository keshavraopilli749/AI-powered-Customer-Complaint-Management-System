# 18. GLOSSARY

A quick reference guide for both pharmaceutical domain terms and software engineering terms used in this project.

## Pharmaceutical Terms

*   **AE (Adverse Event):** Any untoward medical occurrence in a patient or clinical investigation subject administered a pharmaceutical product. It does not necessarily have a causal relationship with the treatment.
*   **API (Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient):** The primary biologically active component of a drug.
*   **APQR (Annual Product Quality Review):** A mandated annual report summarizing all batches, deviations, complaints, and changes for a specific drug product to verify the consistency of the existing process.
*   **Batch / Lot:** A specific quantity of material produced in a process or series of processes so that it is expected to be homogeneous within specified limits.
*   **CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action):** The process of investigating and fixing a problem (Corrective) and ensuring it doesn't recur (Preventive).
*   **Deviation:** A departure from an approved instruction or established standard.
*   **EMA (European Medicines Agency):** The EU agency responsible for the evaluation and supervision of medicinal products.
*   **FDA (Food and Drug Administration):** The US federal agency responsible for drug regulation.
*   **FDF (Finished Dosage Form):** The final physical form of a drug (tablet, capsule, etc.).
*   **GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices):** The minimum standard that a medicines manufacturer must meet in their production processes.
*   **GxP:** Good 'x' Practice (e.g., GMP, GLP, GCP).
*   **PQC (Product Quality Complaint):** A complaint specifically regarding the physical, chemical, or biological attributes of a product.
*   **QMS (Quality Management System):** A formalized system that documents processes, procedures, and responsibilities for achieving quality policies and objectives.
*   **RCA (Root Cause Analysis):** A method of problem-solving used for identifying the root causes of faults or problems.
*   **QP (Qualified Person):** A technical and legal term (especially in the EU) for the individual responsible for certifying that a batch of medicine is fit for release to the market.

## Software & Compliance Terms

*   **21 CFR Part 11:** The FDA regulations on electronic records and electronic signatures (ERES). Requires systems to have audit trails, validated security, and unique e-signatures.
*   **Audit Trail:** A secure, computer-generated, time-stamped electronic record that allows for reconstruction of the course of events relating to the creation, modification, or deletion of an electronic record.
*   **eQMS (Electronic Quality Management System):** A software platform that digitizes QMS processes.
*   **JWT (JSON Web Token):** A standard used for securely transmitting information between parties as a JSON object, typically used for authentication.
*   **LLM (Large Language Model):** A type of AI algorithm that uses deep learning techniques and massively large data sets to understand, summarize, generate and predict new content (e.g., Llama 3, GPT-4).
*   **NLP (Natural Language Processing):** A branch of AI that gives computers the ability to understand text and spoken words in much the same way human beings can.
*   **OCR (Optical Character Recognition):** Technology that recognizes text within a digital image (like a scanned PDF or a photo).
*   **pgvector:** An open-source vector similarity search extension for PostgreSQL, used for AI embeddings.
*   **RBAC (Role-Based Access Control):** A method of restricting network or system access based on the roles of individual users within an enterprise.
*   **SSO (Single Sign-On):** An authentication scheme that allows a user to log in with a single ID and password to any of several related, yet independent, software systems.
