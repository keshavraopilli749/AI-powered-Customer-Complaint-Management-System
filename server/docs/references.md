# 19. REFERENCES

Official documentation and high-quality resources used for the research and planning of this software system.

## FDA Regulations & Guidances
*   **21 CFR Part 211.198 (Complaint Files):** The primary regulatory mandate for pharmaceutical complaint handling in the United States.
    *   *Source:* [FDA CFR Title 21](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfcfr/CFRSearch.cfm?fr=211.198)
*   **21 CFR Part 11 (Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures):** The strict requirements for building compliant software systems for the FDA.
    *   *Source:* [FDA CFR Title 21 Part 11](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfcfr/CFRsearch.cfm?CFRPart=11)
*   **FDA Guidance for Industry: Part 11, Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures — Scope and Application:**
    *   *Source:* [FDA.gov](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/part-11-electronic-records-electronic-signatures-scope-and-application)

## International Guidelines
*   **ICH Q9 (Quality Risk Management):** International Council for Harmonisation guidelines on assessing risk in pharmaceutical quality systems.
    *   *Source:* [ICH.org](https://www.ich.org/page/quality-guidelines)
*   **ICH Q10 (Pharmaceutical Quality System):** Guidelines describing a model for an effective quality management system for the pharmaceutical industry.
    *   *Source:* [ICH.org](https://www.ich.org/page/quality-guidelines)
*   **WHO Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP):** Global standards for medicine production.
    *   *Source:* [WHO.int](https://www.who.int/teams/health-product-policy-and-standards/standards-and-specifications/gmp)
